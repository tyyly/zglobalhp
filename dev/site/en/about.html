      Company Profile

      Company

      Z&GLOBAL Co., Ltd. is a chauffeur and taxi company based in Inzai, Chiba, founded in 2018. We provide Haneda and Narita airport transfers, executive transport, sightseeing charters and golf transfers in the Alphard, Granace and Hiace, and run taxis in the Narita and Inzai area. We serve customers in Japanese, English, Chinese and Korean.
