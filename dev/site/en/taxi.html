      Taxi Services

      Six Ways to Ride

      Booked Pickups

      We pick you up at the place and time you choose: your home, a station such as Chiba New Town Chuo, Inzai-Makinohara or Narita, a hotel or a hospital, so you arrive on time for your morning commute or an important appointment.

      Taxi to Narita Airport

      From the Narita and Inzai area to Narita Airport. Early-morning and late-night flights are covered with an advance booking. If you have a lot of luggage, such as suitcases, please let us know when you book.

      Hospital Visits & Shopping

      A dependable ride for regular hospital visits and shopping. You can also book a pickup for your return trip. Fare discounts are available for holders of a disability certificate.

      Local Sightseeing

      Explore the Hokuso area, including Naritasan Shinshoji Temple and the historic streets of Sawara. Even for a short outing during a flight layover, tell us where you would like to go and how much time you have, and we will give you a fare estimate.

      Corporate & Business

      For meeting clients and moving your staff. Corporate customers can also pay later, with rides billed together on one invoice. Please feel free to contact us.

      Booking in Your Language

      We also take bookings in English, Chinese and Korean. Leave rides for overseas guests and their families to us, starting with confirming the destination.

      Fares & Payment

      Fares, Charges & Payment

      Metered Fares

      Fares are distance-based meter fares approved by the national government. You pay the amount shown on the meter, which reflects the distance and time travelled.

      Additional Charges

      A pickup fee or booking fee may apply when we come to collect you or when you book in advance. A late-night and early-morning surcharge applies from 22:00 to 5:00. Toll road and parking fees are paid by the customer.

      Disability Discount

      Show a disability certificate when you board to receive a fare discount. Please call us to check which certificates are eligible.

      Payment Methods

      You can pay in cash. Corporate customers can also pay later by invoice. For other payment methods, please ask when you book.

      Tell us your pickup point and destination, and we will give you a fare estimate by phone.

      Safety

      Our Commitment to Safety

      Roll Calls and Alcohol Tests Every Shift

      Every day, an operations manager conducts a roll call and an alcohol test at the start and end of each driver's shift.

      Daily Vehicle Inspections

      We inspect every vehicle before it leaves the depot, so you ride in a well-maintained car.

      Oversight by Operations Managers

      Operations managers check each driver's health and working and rest hours, so that every shift is run safely and without strain.

      FAQ

      Frequently Asked Questions

      Can I book a taxi in advance?

      Yes. We accept bookings by phone or LINE. Please tell us the date and time, pickup point, destination and number of passengers.

      Which areas do you serve?

      Our service area is the Hokuso traffic zone, centred on Narita and Inzai (we also pick up at stations such as Chiba New Town Chuo, Inzai-Makinohara, Kioroshi and Narita). You can also ride from within the zone to destinations outside it, such as central Tokyo or Haneda Airport.

      Can I take a taxi to Narita Airport early in the morning or late at night?

      Yes, with an advance booking. If your time is fixed, we recommend booking by the day before.

      How can I pay?

      You can pay in cash. Corporate customers can also pay later by invoice. For other payment methods, please ask when you book.

      Can I book in a language other than Japanese?

      Yes. We also take bookings in English, Chinese and Korean, by phone or LINE.

      What if I leave something in the taxi?

      Please call us at 0476-29-5498 and tell us the date and time of your ride, where you got on and off, and a description of the item.
